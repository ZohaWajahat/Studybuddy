Mathematics

Mathematics is the formal study of numbers, shapes, logic, quantities, and arrangements. Derived from the Greek word máthema, meaning "learning" or "knowledge", it operates as a universal language to discover patterns, establish absolute Truths through logical proofs, and quantify the physical world.
