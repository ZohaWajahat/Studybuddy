    At StudyBuddy, your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services. By accessing or using StudyBuddy, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

    We collect information to improve our services and enhance your learning experience. The types of information we may collect include:


    A. Personal Information


    • Name, email address, and contact details when you register or sign up.

    B. Non Personal Information


    • Device information, browser type, and IP address.

    We use your information to provide, improve, and personalize our services. Specifically, we may use your data for the following purposes:


    • To process your registration and manage your account.

    • For Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.
    • With Service Providers: We work with trusted third parties to manage customer support, and improve our services.

    4. Data Security


    We take data security seriously and implement measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include:


    • Encryption of sensitive data.
    • Regular monitoring of our systems for potential vulnerabilities.
    • Secure storage and limited access to personal information.

    5. Your Rights


    You have the following rights regarding your personal information:


    • Access and Update: View and edit your account information at any time.
    • Delete Your Data: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
    • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from promotional emails or adjust cookie preferences.
